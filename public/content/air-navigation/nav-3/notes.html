How to use this collection

  1. Read one chapter at a time and work through its examples before moving on.
  2. Keep the individual chapter open when revising a particular chart topic.
  3. Use the collection download when you want the same lessons together for offline study.
  4. Return to the chapter's practice questions for the material available in its own question bank.

The individual lessons remain available at their established links. This collection organises their existing teaching material rather than replacing it with a new set of answers.
