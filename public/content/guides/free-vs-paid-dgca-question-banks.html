Every month a cadet asks me the same question in slightly different words: should I pay for a DGCA question bank, or is free enough? The marketing pages answer with rankings and pass-rate screenshots. I will not. Here is the honest split — what you actually need to clear papers, what free self-study can cover, and when paying for live classes is worth it.

What a DGCA question bank is supposed to do

A question bank is not a syllabus. It is a practice tool. Its job is to put you under the same pressure the hall will: multiple-choice questions, a clock, and the habit of eliminating wrong options. If a bank only gives you PDFs of remembered papers with no explanations, you are memorising shapes — and DGCA rewords questions often enough that shape-memory fails.

What you actually need from any bank, paid or free:

  • Coverage of the real papers — Navigation (with Radio Nav and Instruments inside it), Technical General, Meteorology, Air Regulations, Technical Specific, and RTR(A) where it applies.
  • Explanations you can trust — not a green tick with silence. Wrong answers taught to a student pilot are a safety problem, not a typo. See how answers are verified on this site.
  • Timed, full-length practice — chapter quizzes alone will not prepare you for a 100-question Navigation paper. You need exam-mode papers and previous-year papers under the clock.

Free vs paid — the real trade-offs

Paid banks are not automatically better. Free banks are not automatically worse. The trade-offs I see in practice:

Why Ghost Aviator keeps self-study free

Ground study should not be the part of CPL training that bankrupts a cadet. Flying hours dominate the bill — see pilot training cost in India. Charging again for notes and mocks on top of that is a choice other platforms make. This one does not.

Everything for self-study stays free permanently: chapter notes, the question bank, past papers, mock exams, video lectures, and the RTR(A) simulator. That is site policy, not a launch discount.

The only paid part is optional live online ground classes with me — small batches, subject by subject, for students who want a live instructor rather than (or in addition to) solo study. Prices and batches are on /live-classes. If you never join a live batch, you still keep every self-study tool.

When I tell a student to pay. Pay for flying training you cannot skip. Pay for a Class 1 medical you cannot skip. Pay for a type rating when an airline needs it. Pay for live classes only if you are stuck — weak Navigation under the clock, or you need someone to force a schedule. Do not pay for another PDF dump of “remembered” questions you cannot verify.

A practical way to decide

  1. Open CPL subjects (or ATPL if that is your track) and work one chapter end-to-end: notes, quiz, then review mistakes.
  2. Sit one full paper in exam mode under the clock. If you are consistently near the pass mark after honest practice, you do not need a paid bank.
  3. If the same chapters keep failing and self-study is not fixing it, consider a live batch for that subject — not another subscription you will not open.

Free is enough for the self-study half of the job. Paid is optional coaching, not a second question bank tax. Start with the free tools; upgrade only where you are actually stuck.
