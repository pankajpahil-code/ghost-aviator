Use this guide to navigate the DGCA ATPL (Aeroplanes) syllabus and the Ghost Aviator reference library.

Use the DGCA syllabus as the starting point

The ATPL study library is organised around CAR Section 7, Series B, Part VI for ATPL (Aeroplanes). Its eight syllabus areas are Air Navigation; Aviation Meteorology; Radio Aids and Instruments (Avionics); Air Regulation; Aircraft and Engines; Technical Specific; Technical Performance; and Radio Telephony.

These are the areas listed in the CAR, not eight examinations that every applicant must sit. The papers applicable to you depend on your application, existing qualifications and aircraft requirements. Confirm them through DGCA and your PARIKSHA profile.

Study in a clear sequence

  1. Identify your scope. Read the relevant CAR appendix and current examination notices before making a study plan.
  2. Choose a topic and read. Each ATPL study area groups the shared CPL / ATPL notes by topic. These chapters were prepared with both tracks in mind. Read them directly within ATPL, then use the official appendix to plan the rest of your study.
  3. Practise separately. All existing question banks previously shown under ATPL are now in the CPL study section. Their questions and answer keys have been retained; their relocation does not certify them as a complete CPL or ATPL examination bank.
  4. Prepare to explain. Use the syllabus to identify gaps and practise explaining your reasoning. Consult current approved documentation for aircraft limitations, performance and procedures.

Supporting references

Study Human Performance within the human-factors and physiology topics in ATPL Air Regulation, and mass, balance and aeroplane performance within Technical Performance. The full Human Performance book and free Ghost Aviator books remain available too.
