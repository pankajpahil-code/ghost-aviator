CPL and ATPL licences are valid for 5 years (ATPL sometimes taught as 2).

That is out of date.

TEN years for ATPL, CPL and PPL. The Student Pilot Licence is still 5.

Source: Rule 39C, Aircraft Rules 1937, as amended by G.S.R. 733(E) dated 10 October 2023 (Aircraft (First Amendment) Rules, 2023) — 'Five years' substituted by 'Ten years' against ATPL and CPL.

Medical validity is unchanged: 12 months ATPL/CPL, halved to 6 past age 40. Instrument Rating is 12 months and is a RATING under Schedule II, not a licence validity — do not fold it in.
