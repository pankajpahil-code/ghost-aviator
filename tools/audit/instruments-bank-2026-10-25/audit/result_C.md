## Notes on items checked and found OK, where a reviewer might hesitate

- **Autoland heights (AUTOFLIGHT\|51/62/86/98/104).** The worked sequence: RADALT live at 2500 ft; LOC and G/S captured before 1500 ft (LAND 3, flare armed); stabiliser nose-up trim at 330 ft; G/S disconnected and flare at 50 ft; touchdown/roll-out mode at about 5 ft. The keys and the "flare armed about 1500 ft" wording are consistent with it.
- **ENGINE\|68, re-worked.** Below critical altitude the engine holds rated boost: MAP = 14.7 + 10 = about 24.7 psi; × 2.036 = 50.3 inHg. Key a (50 inHg) is correct. Option d ("ambient + 10 psi") is wrong, because boost is referenced to standard sea-level pressure.
- **AUTOFLIGHT\|131.** In an ISA troposphere, a constant-Mach climb gives falling TAS (falling speed of sound) and falling CAS. Key b is correct, and c is false below the tropopause.
- **FUEL\|7.** Compensated capacitive system: the worked text says temperature effects are "self cancelling" (density and dielectric constant rise as the level falls), so there is no change. Same as specimen Q49 (answer B, remained the same).
- **ENGINE\|43/46.** Parallel EGT thermocouples: specimen Q51, answer D, "largely unaffected".
- **TCAS symbols and advisories (TCAS\|9-14, 16, 18, 21, 28, 29, 50).** These match the worked texts.
